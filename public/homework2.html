A Guide to Baking Brownies

A guide to bake classic, rich, and fudgy home made brownies.

Essential ingredients

Wet ingerdients

  • 1/2 cup of Un-salted buttter
  • 2 Eggs
  • One teaspoon of pure vanilla extract

Dry ingredients

  • 1 cup of granulated white sugar
  • 1/3 cup of un-sweetened cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup of all-purpose flower
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon baking powder

Step-By-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat and Prep

    2. Heat your oven to 350°F or 175°C. Line an 8x8-inch metal baking pan with parchment paper, leaving a little overhang for a easy removal.

  2. Melt the Butter

    3. Place the 1/2 cup of un-salted butter in a bowl, then melt it in the microwave. After let it cool for 2-3 minutes.

  3. Mix Butter and Sugar

    4. Pour the warm melted butter into a large mixing bowl, then add the white sugar and wisk vigorously untill smooth and glossy.

  4. Add Wet Ingredients

    5. Whisk the 2 large eggs and 1 teaspoon of vanilla extarct untill the mixture looks uniform and slightly thick.

  5. Combine Dry Ingerdients

    6. Sift in the ⅓ cup cocoa powder, ½ cup all-purpose flour, ¼ teaspoon salt, and ¼ teaspoon baking powder.

  6. Fold the Batter

    7. Gently stir with a spatula just until the flour disappears. Do not overmix, or the brownies will turn out cakey instead of fudgy.

  7. Bake

    8. Spread the batter evenly into your prepared pan. Bake on the center rack for 20 to 25 minutes. A toothpick inserted into the center should come out with a few moist crumbs attached.

  8. Let it Rest

    9. Remove from the pan after baking and let it rest on a wire rack for about 10-15 minutes.

Lastly, Enjoy Your Homemade Brownies!
